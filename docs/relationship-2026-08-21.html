Journal of family psychology : JFP : journal of the Division of Family Psychology of the American Psychological Association (Division 43) · Hope for our future: Relational hope promotes relationship goal pursuit and well-being.

關係希望（relational hope）顯著促進伴侶在目標對話中對目標的正向評價，並預測三個月後目標進展與關係福祉的長期提升，此效應透過更正向地視關係目標而中介，且不僅由樂觀或關係滿足感所能解釋。
